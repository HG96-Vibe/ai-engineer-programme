The career

What the job is, what it pays, and how you get in.

We designed this programme by reading UK AI engineer job adverts, not by guessing. This page is what we found, including the parts that are not good news.

It is a software job with an AI specialism

The clearest finding from the adverts: AI engineer is a software engineering role. Shipping to production, building APIs, deployment pipelines, and system design come up more often than any single AI technique. Knowledge of AI is the specialism layered on top.

The good news for a beginner is that this is learnable. You are not being asked to invent models or understand research papers. You are being asked to connect a model to a company's data and tools, make sure it behaves, and ship it so people can use it.

The words employers use

Production-grade. Agentic workflows. Retrieval-augmented generation. Evaluation. Guardrails. Monitoring. Measurable business value. End to end. The programme uses these words from Stage 1, so that you describe your work the way the adverts describe the job.

The junior market, honestly

This is the part most training providers leave out.

It is small

Fewer than one in five adverts was for a junior. Most of the market is mid-level and senior. That is normal for a new specialism, and it means a beginner needs evidence, not just enthusiasm.

It often asks for a degree

Most junior adverts list a degree. A career changer's only lever against that is a portfolio: a deployed, evaluated application that a graduate with no projects cannot show. That is why the programme is built around one.

It is full of fake vacancies

Search "junior AI engineer" on a big UK job board and much of what comes back is paid training courses dressed up as jobs. Stage 10 teaches you to spot them so you do not waste months applying to adverts that were never jobs.

What a realistic first role looks like

Most graduates of this programme will not walk into a role titled "AI Engineer" on day one. Their first role is more likely to be a Junior AI Developer, an AI Automation Engineer, a graduate technology role with an AI focus, or a developer or automation role with an AI component, often on the Microsoft stack, often outside London.

Stated junior pay in the adverts we analysed was typically in the low-to-mid thirty-thousands, higher in London. Mid-level AI engineer roles, two to three years on, were advertised at roughly double that. The skills are contiguous: what you learn here is the same work, done with more responsibility.

What we will not tell you

We will not tell you that this programme leads directly to a £60,000+ AI engineer role. Nobody honest can, because that is not how the UK market works for people starting from zero. What we can tell you is that the capability is real, the portfolio is real, and the path from a first role to that title is well-trodden.

Why a career changer can still do this

Because the job is new enough that the pool of people who can actually do it is small, and the evidence that you can do it is something you can build yourself, at home, in months. A deployed application that answers questions from real documents, takes real actions, has been tested properly, and runs at a public URL is something most applicants, including many graduates, cannot show. The programme exists to make sure you can.
